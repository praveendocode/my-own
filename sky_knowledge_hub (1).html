System Overview

What is Sky DE & AT?

Sky Deutschland and Sky Austria provide digital subscription television services across Germany (DE) and Austria (AT).

Reception Types:
  • Internet (Sky Stream)
  • Satellite
  • Cable
Content Portfolio: Entertainment, Movies, Series, Sports, Bundesliga, Kids content, Add-ons, Third-party streaming services

Core Principles

  • Base product (Entertainment) is MANDATORY in all subscriptions
  • Cannot purchase base product alone—must bundle with premium product
  • Initial contracts are 12 months minimum
  • Reception type determines available hardware and add-ons

Information Architecture Design Rationale

Why This Structure Works:

  • Cognitive Load Management: Separates customer-facing (Digital) from agent-facing (Assisted) journeys
  • Role-Based Access: Different roles see relevant information first (CSRs vs QA vs Developers)
  • Progressive Disclosure: Overview → Products → Journeys → Decision Logic → Validation
  • Domain Language: Uses exact Sky terminology (Upsell, Recommitment, Retention)
  • Scanability: Visual cards and icons enable quick navigation

Digital Journeys (Customer Self-Service)

Digital Journeys
├── 1. Initial Sales
│   ├── When: New customer, no Sky account
│   ├── Contract: 12 months minimum
│   ├── Products: Base + Premium bundle
│   └── Result: New customer account created
│
├── 2. Upsell
│   ├── When: Active customer wants more
│   ├── Contract: Monthly cancellable
│   ├── Products: Additional products only
│   └── Result: Immediate activation
│
├── 3. Recommitment
│   ├── When: Near/out of contract
│   ├── Contract: 12 or 24 months
│   ├── Options: Stay/Upgrade/Downgrade
│   └── Result: New commitment period
│
├── 4. Retention
│   ├── When: Cancellation already placed
│   ├── Contract: 12 or 24 months
│   ├── Options: Modify products
│   └── Result: Cancellation reversed
│
└── 5. Sky TV Journeys
    ├── Sky TV Sales (hardware only)
    ├── Sky TV Multiscreen Upsell
    ├── Sky TV Base Pack Swap (→ premium)
    ├── Sky TV Recommitment
    └── Sky TV Retention

Journey Selection Logic

  • Initial Sales: Customer email not in system → Create account + Purchase
  • Upsell: Active customer + No cancellation → Add products (monthly)
  • Recommitment: Within 3 months of contract end OR out of contract → New commitment
  • Retention: Standard cancellation exists → Cancel the cancellation

Recommitment Activation Logic (12 Months)

Scenario Activation Type Reason
Stay where you are (no changes) Future-dated No immediate change needed
Recommitment with upgrade Immediate Adding products requires instant activation
Recommitment with downgrade Future-dated Removing products respects current contract
Recommitment with upgrade + downgrade Immediate Upgrades take priority

Assisted Journeys (Agent via Salesforce)

Key Differences from Digital

  • Agent executes on behalf of customer
  • Payment: Direct Debit ONLY (no credit card/PayPal)
  • Sky TV Initial Sales: NOT available in assisted flow
  • Requires email verification step

Leave Journeys

Leave Journeys
├── 1. Standard Cancellation
│   ├── When: Customer wants to end subscription
│   ├── Effective: End of contract period
│   └── Example: Contract 1.1.2025-31.12.2025
│       Cancel on 1.7.2025 → Disconnects 31.12.2025
│
├── 2. Downgrade
│   ├── When: Remove specific products, keep others
│   ├── Logic: Each product follows own runtime
│   └── Example:
│       Bundle (12-month) → Disconnects at contract end
│       Netflix (1-month) → Disconnects today + 30 days - 1
│
└── 3. Revocation
    ├── Order Revocation (within 14 days)
    │   └── Full refund applicable
    └── Extraordinary Revocation (after 14 days)
        └── No refund

Downgrade Disconnection Logic

Formula for Monthly Products:
Disconnection Date = Today + 30 days - 1 day
Formula for 12-Month Products:
Disconnection Date = Original Contract End Date

Revocation Rules (Germany)

  • Customer has 14-day legal right to revoke orders
  • Within 14 days: Full refund + order cancelled
  • After 14 days: No refund (Extraordinary Revocation)
  • If initial purchase revoked: Customer status becomes "Idle"
  • Access: Tier 3 → Add → Under Actions → Order Revocation

CARE Operations

CARE Journeys
├── Reception Type Change (RTC)
│   ├── Changes: Satellite ↔ Cable ↔ Internet
│   ├── Result: Old hardware disconnected
│   └── Process: Actions → Add → RTC → Select Type
│
├── Relocation
│   ├── When: Customer moving address
│   ├── Captures: Relocation date
│   └── Result: Technician scheduled
│
├── RTC + Relocation Combined
│   ├── When: Moving + changing reception
│   └── Updates: Address + Reception Type
│
├── Reshipment
│   ├── When: Hardware not received
│   ├── Condition: Shipment already dispatched
│   └── Result: New hardware sent
│
└── Defect Swap
    ├── When: Hardware defective
    ├── Process: Create case → Upload article → Link
    └── Result: Defective item replaced

Reception Type Change Impact

From To Disconnected New Hardware
Satellite Internet Q Receiver, Smartcard, HD+ Sky Stream Box
Cable Satellite Cable receiver Q Receiver, Smartcard
Internet Satellite Stream Box Q Receiver, Smartcard

System Change Closure

  • RTC, Relocation, and RTC+Relocation create open system changes
  • System changes remain open until batch job completes
  • Production: Batch runs once per day
  • Lower environments: Manual batch execution required
  • Once closed: Order status updates to "Completed"

Decision Trees

Sales & Upgrade Decision Flow

START: Customer Contact
│
├─❌ Does customer have Sky account?
│   └─→ NO → INITIAL SALES
│
├─✅ YES → Is customer buying Sky TV hardware only?
│   ├─→ YES → SKY TV SALES
│   └─→ NO → Continue
│
├─→ Does customer want additional products?
│   ├─→ YES → UPSELL
│   └─→ NO → Continue
│
├─→ Is customer out of contract or near end?
│   ├─→ YES → RECOMMITMENT
│   └─→ NO → Continue
│
└─→ Has customer placed cancellation?
    └─→ YES → RETENTION

Leave Journey Decision Flow

START: Customer Wants to Leave
│
├─→ Does customer want to leave completely?
│   └─→ YES → STANDARD CANCELLATION
│
├─→ Does customer want to remove specific products?
│   └─→ YES → DOWNGRADE
│
├─→ Is order within 14 days?
│   ├─→ YES → ORDER REVOCATION (Refund)
│   └─→ NO → EXTRAORDINARY REVOCATION (No Refund)

CARE Journey Decision Flow

START: Customer Needs Support
│
├─→ Is customer changing reception method?
│   └─→ YES → RECEPTION TYPE CHANGE
│
├─→ Is customer moving address?
│   ├─→ YES + Changing Reception → RTC + RELOCATION
│   └─→ YES (No RTC) → RELOCATION
│
├─→ Is hardware missing after dispatch?
│   └─→ YES → RESHIPMENT
│
└─→ Is hardware defective?
    └─→ YES → DEFECT SWAP

Campaign Management

Campaign Promotion Rules

  • Campaign promotions applicable ONLY for: Upsell, Recommitment, Retention
  • One campaign promotion per journey per customer
  • Notification displayed in FE when campaign linked
  • Requires "Marketing User" access in Salesforce

Campaign Creation Process

Campaign Setup Steps
│
1. Access Campaigns
   └─→ App Launcher → Search "Campaigns"
│
2. Create Campaign Object
   ├─→ Click "New" button
   ├─→ Enter Campaign Name
   ├─→ Check "Active" checkbox
   ├─→ Select Type: Upsell/Recommitment/Retention
   ├─→ Set Start Date (past date)
   ├─→ Set End Date (future date)
   └─→ Click Save
│
3. Link Promotion to Campaign
   ├─→ Campaign Promotion Junctions → "New"
   ├─→ Enter Campaign Promotion Junction Record name
   ├─→ Search and select promo code
   └─→ Click Save
│
4. Add Customers to Campaign
   ├─→ Campaign Members → "Add Contacts"
   ├─→ Select customers to link
   ├─→ Click Next
   └─→ Click Submit
│
5. Verify in Customer Journey
   └─→ Login as customer → Check for notification

Campaign Linking Impact

Before Campaign Link: Standard product prices shown
After Campaign Link: Campaign notification banner appears with promotional pricing
